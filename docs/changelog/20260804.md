# 20260804

**First public release.** 2026-08-04. [Download](https://github.com/Keyaku/gpd-xdplus-customrom/releases/tag/20260804) · zip `lineage-18.1-20260804-UNOFFICIAL-xdplus.zip`.

Unofficial Android 11 for the GPD XD+, a device that shipped with Android 7.0 in 2018 and was left there. This release is the point where the port became usable as a daily handheld rather than a project, and where the sources became public.

## At a glance

- **LineageOS 18.1 (Android 11)** on a 2018 device that shipped with Android 7.0, release-signed, kernel built from public GPL source. [Details](#platform)
- **PowerVR GX6250 hardware acceleration**, OpenGL ES and Vulkan, with a compatibility layer and a persistent shader cache. [Details](#graphics)
- **mini-HDMI mirroring** to an external monitor, experimental and behind a toggle. [Details](#graphics)
- **Controller and D-Pad fully mapped**, speakers, headphone jack with detection, and the microphone. [Details](#input-audio-connectivity)
- **Wi-Fi including 5 GHz 802.11ac**, and **Bluetooth including A2DP**. [Details](#input-audio-connectivity)
- **Standby drain about 0.34 %/hour** with Wi-Fi on. [Details](#input-audio-connectivity)
- **TEE-backed keymaster and gatekeeper** and **Widevine L3**, so a PIN and DRM playback work. [Details](#security-and-system)
- **An in-Settings "GPD XD+" menu** for the device-specific toggles. [Details](#security-and-system)
- **Configured as a SIM-less, camera-less handheld** — camera removed, telephony dormant, no Google apps. [Details](#security-and-system)
- ⚠️ **Known limits**: SELinux permissive, `/data` unencrypted, OTA auto-install unverified, WPA3/SAE will not connect. [Details](#notes-for-this-release)

## Platform

- LineageOS 18.1 (Android 11), release-signed, `user` variant.
- Kernel **3.18.79 built from public GPL source**, not a prebuilt lifted from another image.
- Boot to usable in about 30 seconds.
- Configured as a **SIM-less, camera-less handheld**, which is what the hardware is.

## Graphics

- **PowerVR GX6250 hardware acceleration**, OpenGL ES and **Vulkan** both, on the 8.1-era vendor driver.
- A Vulkan compatibility layer for the ways that driver differs from what Android 11 expects — without it, Vulkan games crash on startup or lose their compiled shaders on every launch.
- A **persistent pipeline cache**, so a game pays its shader compile once instead of on every launch, with a compile-progress notification while it happens.
- **mini-HDMI mirroring** to an external monitor, both pipelines on hardware overlays at 60 Hz. Experimental and behind a toggle: the built-in panel does not render correctly while mirroring.

## Input, audio, connectivity

- Controller and **D-Pad** fully mapped, including a fix for crossed Select and menu keys.
- Speakers, headphone jack with **detection**, and the **microphone** — the last needed an audio HAL fix before recording produced anything but silence.
- **Wi-Fi**, including **5 GHz 802.11ac**, with recovery from a chip reset that used to take the interface down permanently.
- **Bluetooth**, including A2DP audio — fixed by a kernel change to how HCI writes are framed.
- Standby drain brought down to about **0.34 %/hour** with Wi-Fi on.

## Security and system

- **TEE-backed keymaster and gatekeeper**, which is what makes a PIN and hardware-backed keys work.
- **Widevine** DRM HAL brought up on the 8.1-era blobs (L3).
- An in-Settings **"GPD XD+"** menu for the device-specific toggles.
- Camera app, camera feature flags and camera blobs removed; telephony dormant; no Google apps.
- Vendor partition slimmed from 141 MB to 73 MB by dropping blobs this device cannot reach.

## Notes for this release

- **SELinux is permissive.**
- **`/data` is not encrypted.**
- **OTA auto-install is unverified** — the Updater downloads and verifies a build, but install the zip from TWRP until the automatic path is confirmed on hardware.
- **WPA3/SAE will not connect**; the limit is in the closed-source Wi-Fi driver.
- TWRP **3.7.0_11-0**, built for this device, is the recommended recovery.
